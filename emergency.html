First-Aid Protocols & Live Timers

Step-by-step triage actions and live intervention timers for critical medical scenarios.

Medically Verified Standard
HIGH PRIORITY

Severe Bleeding / Cut

Arterial or deep laceration hemorrhage control.

  1. Apply firm, direct pressure with clean cloth/gauze.
  2. Elevate limb above heart level if possible.
  3. If blood soaks through, add more layers—do not remove initial cloth.
  4. Apply tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound if life-threatening bleeding persists.
Pressure Hold Clock 10:00
CRITICAL

Cardiac Arrest / CPR

Unresponsive patient without normal breathing.

  1. Place hands on center of chest. Push hard & fast (100-120 bpm).
  2. 30 chest compressions followed by 2 rescue breaths.
  3. Use AED (Defibrillator) as soon as available.
2-Min CPR Cycle Clock 02:00
MODERATE TO SEVERE

Thermal / Chemical Burns

Heat, steam, electrical or chemical exposure.

  • Cool with clean running water for at least 15-20 minutes.
  • Do NOT use ice, butter, or toothpaste on burns.
  • Cover loosely with sterile non-stick bandage or plastic wrap.
Cooling Water Timer 20:00
CRITICAL TOXIN

Snakebite & Envenomation

Reptile or toxic creature venom exposure.

  • Keep patient calm & completely still to slow venom spread.
  • Immobilize bitten limb below heart level.
  • DON'T: Cut wound, suck venom, or apply tourniquet.
TRAUMA

Bone Fracture / Dislocation

Deformity, severe pain, inability to move limb.

  • Immobilize the area using splints or padding in position found.
  • Apply cold pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling.
  • Do NOT attempt to realign broken bones manually.
AIRWAY EMERGENCY

Choking (Heimlich Maneuver)

Inability to speak, cough or breathe.

  • Give 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades.
  • Perform 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) inwards & upwards above navel.
  • Alternate until object dislodges or patient becomes unconscious.
