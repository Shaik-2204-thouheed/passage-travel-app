Meta Safety AI Guidelines & Anti-Hostage Protocol

Interactive AI advisory providing safety tactics during vehicle robbery or terror threats.

During Armed Robbery

  • Remain calm and do not attempt aggressive physical confrontation.
  • Use Silent Panic SOS on GuardianTransit to alert AP Police without drawing attention.
  • Memorize perpetrator descriptions and vehicle direction of transit.

Terror / Hostage Situation

  • Activate Terror SOS button to start low-bandwidth silent live location stream.
  • Keep phone hidden; system will send periodic GPS updates to MHA 112 desk.
  • Stay low below vehicle window line if gunfire is heard outside.
